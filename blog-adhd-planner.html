The ADHD-Friendly Planner That Shows You One Task at a Time

If you have ADHD, or an ADHD-style brain, you know the feeling: you open your planner, you see 20 tasks, and you do none of them. It is not laziness. It is overload. A long list is not a plan — it is a wall.

OneStep was built for that exact moment. Instead of a long list, it shows you one thing: your next step. You do it, you check it, and then you see the next one. You can have up to 3 tasks for today — no more on the Home screen. The rest wait in Week view, where they belong, until their day comes.

Habits work the same way: check them in seconds, with no long setup. And if you miss a day, your streak pauses. It never resets to zero, and it never makes you feel like you failed. You just pick it up again.

OneStep works offline, and your data stays on your phone by default. There are no ads, and no subscription trap. If you choose to create an account, your tasks can back up — but you never have to. Guest mode is the default.

OneStep is not medical advice, and it does not treat ADHD. It is a planner that respects how an overwhelmed brain actually works: one thing at a time.

If long lists freeze you, try picking one next step today. Just one. That is a good day.
