Privacy Policy for OneStep

OneStep is a daily planner. It works offline and keeps your tasks, habits, and reminders on your device by default.

What we collect

If you use OneStep without an account, we collect nothing from you. Your data stays on your phone.

If you choose to create an account, we collect your email address so you can sign in, and we store a backup of your tasks and habits in Firebase (Google Firebase, project onestep-acb33) so you can restore them. No other personal data is collected.

What we do not do

No ads, no sale of your data, no sharing your data with advertisers, and no tracking for advertising.

Your choices

Use OneStep fully without an account. Back up now, or download your backup, from Account & Backup in the app. Sign out at any time — your local tasks stay on your phone. Delete your account from Account > Delete account. This deletes your synced backup and your account. Your local tasks stay on your phone unless you delete them there.

Notifications

Reminders are off by default. OneStep only sends a reminder if you choose one for a specific task, with at most one gentle follow-up the same day, and never between 10pm and 7am.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact: onestep.help@outlook.com
